TFA Certification Notice

Become a Certified Trivia Administrator

Certification Overview

The Trivia Fairness Association invites qualified individuals to apply for certification as a TFA Certified Trivia Administrator, a role defined not by authority, but by an uncompromising commitment to fairness.

This is not a simple application. It is a multi-stage evaluation of judgment, integrity, and impartiality.

Applicants will undergo:

  • Rigorous scenario-based fairness testing
  • Observed administration review
  • Panel evaluation and final council determination

Timeline: 90-180+ days

Outcome: Highly selective. Many will not advance.

All candidates are subject to:

  • Recorded evaluations
  • Ethical scrutiny
  • Archival of rulings for precedent

Certification is awarded only to those who demonstrate the ability to apply fairness consistently, even when outcomes are unclear, contested, or uncomfortable.

No appeals. No shortcuts. No exceptions.

Administrative Capacity Notice

Due to sustained demand and the Association's longstanding belief that fairness cannot be scaled indefinitely, the number of active TFA Certified Trivia Administrators remains subject to a permanent organizational cap.

This cap was formally established by the Association in 1957 and remains in effect today.

As a result, certification openings may be limited regardless of applicant qualification. Eligible applicants who cannot immediately be accommodated may petition for placement onto the TFA Administrative Waitlist.

Please note:

  • A non-refundable waitlist processing fee of $600 is required for consideration
  • Placement on the waitlist does not guarantee future certification or advancement
  • Wait periods may extend several years depending on attrition, review cycles, and Council determinations

Applicants with documented familial or institutional connections to existing or former TFA administrators may receive legacy priority consideration.

Applicants seeking such consideration are advised to clearly indicate all qualifying relationships within their application materials.

Why Serve

TFA administrators help protect the integrity of organized trivia. They review procedures, support investigations, advise local hosts and leagues, maintain records, and help ensure that fairness standards are applied consistently across events and regions.

The role is not only administrative. It is custodial. TFA administrators help preserve trust in the game itself.

Who Should Apply

This role is well suited for experienced hosts, organizers, league coordinators, question writers, archivists, and community leaders who care deeply about trivia fairness and procedural consistency.

Core Traits

  • Strong judgment and respect for evidence
  • Comfort with documentation and policy review
  • Calm, impartial communication
  • Reliability with confidential or sensitive matters
  • Commitment to fairness over popularity

Administrator Responsibilities

Policy Support

Help interpret and apply TFA standards for gameplay, question quality, scoring, and event certification.

Case Review

Assist in reviewing disputes, procedural complaints, and supporting documentation for appeals and investigations.

Community Guidance

Advise hosts and organizers on how to improve consistency, transparency, and fairness in their events.

Records & Reporting

Maintain written summaries, archived rulings, and internal notes that support long-term continuity and precedent.

Minimum Expectations

  • Demonstrated experience with organized trivia or competition administration
  • Ability to review written materials carefully and objectively
  • Availability for periodic review meetings and council support work
  • Willingness to follow TFA confidentiality and conflict-of-interest standards

What the Process Looks Like

  1. Initial expression of interest and background review
  2. Evaluation of trivia governance or administrative experience
  3. Interview or council screening conversation
  4. Provisional service period with assigned responsibilities

Expedited Review Provision

Applicants may optionally request Expedited Review Status through the Association's Fairness Accessibility Provision.

Unlike the standard certification pathway, which may require 90-180+ days of procedural review, archival examination, and fairness observation, expedited review applicants are typically processed in under two (2) hours.

To ensure equitable administrative efficiency, expedited applicants may bypass:

  • Standard panel deliberations
  • Extended fairness observation periods
  • Multi-stage archival review procedures
  • Certain non-essential adjudicative delays deemed historically ceremonial in nature

Expedited review adjusts procedural requirements and sequencing only and does not officially influence certification outcome or council determinations.

All expedited applicants will receive:

  • Immediate priority placement within the certification queue
  • A PDF version of the Official Certificate of Trivia Fairness Association Certified Trivia Administrator suitable for on-demand printing
  • Official TFA insignia, seals, and archival administrator numbering
  • Provisional recognition within the Association registry pending final archival synchronization

In keeping with the TFA's longstanding commitment to fairness, the expedited administrative fee of $20 has remained unchanged for more than 100 years and may be paid in ten installments over a five-year period.

The Association further recognizes that fairness includes accessibility. As such, expedited review remains available to all qualifying applicants regardless of financial standing.

All candidates remain subject to:

  • Recorded evaluations
  • Ethical scrutiny and fairness observation
  • Archival preservation of rulings for precedent and institutional review
  • Undisclosed fairness testing environments

Certification is awarded only to those who demonstrate the ability to apply fairness consistently, even when outcomes are unclear, contested, uncomfortable, or personally disadvantageous.

Certificate Issuance & Reproduction Rights

Upon successful completion of certification review and confirmation of administrative standing, applicants approved through the expedited review pathway will receive a PDF version of the Official Certificate of the Trivia Fairness Association Certified Trivia Administrator.

This document will be delivered electronically and may be retained for personal archival purposes or printed on demand by the certificate holder.

The certificate package will include:

  • The official TFA seal and insignia
  • Authorized council signatures
  • Administrator identification number
  • Date of certification and standing classification
  • The official TFA logo prominently displayed

Applicants are advised that reproduction of the certificate does not itself confer authority beyond the standing recognized by the Association's active records and archives.

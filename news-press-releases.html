July 18, 2026

TFA Announces 2026 Fair Play Standards Refresh

The Trivia Fairness Association today released an updated fair play guidance package focused on device visibility, score verification timing, and host-side dispute logging for certified events.

The refresh introduces a standardized post-round review window, clearer expectations for audience-submitted correction claims, and stronger documentation language for high-stakes playoff formats.

Press note: The standards refresh will be phased into TFA-certified leagues beginning September 1, 2026, with regional briefings hosted throughout August.

TFA Opens Comment Period on Live Event Technology Guidance

The Association has opened a public comment period on proposed recommendations covering projector-based answer reveals, wireless buzzer latency controls, and moderation practices for hybrid in-person and livestream trivia events.

I'm planning a road trip along the East Coast this summer and would love to visit a few TFA-approved trivia venues along the way.

I'm less interested in the size of the event and more interested in:

* Well-run games

* Strong question writing

* Fair rulings

* Experienced TFA-certified administrators

* Fun regular crowds

What are everyone's favorite East Coast venues?

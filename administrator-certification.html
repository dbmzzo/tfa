Expedited Review Notice

Applicants requesting expedited review may bypass portions of the standard procedural queue and are generally reviewed within approximately 2 hours.

Expedited review does not influence certification outcome, council determinations, or ethical standards.

Applicants approved for expedited processing may receive:

  • Priority queue placement
  • Printable PDF certification materials
  • Official TFA insignia access
  • Archival administrator numbering

The expedited administrative fee is $20 and may be paid in installments.

Meet The Mascot

Hootlet

Hootlet is the official TFA Kidz mascot. Hootlet reminds young players that trivia should be bright, joyful, fair, and full of curiosity.

Whenever Hootlet shows up, it means it is time to listen closely, play honestly, and have a colorful kind of fun.

What Is Trivia Fairness?

Trivia fairness means everyone gets the same question, the same chance to answer, and the same rules. No sneaky clues. No confusing trick wording. No favorites.

Why It Matters

When games are fair, kids can focus on learning, laughing, and trying their best. Fair games feel more exciting because every point is earned.

The TFA Kidz Code

Be Honest

Answer with what you know, not with secret help.

Be Clear

Questions should be easy to understand and fair to everyone playing.

Be Kind

Celebrate great answers and help make the game fun for the whole room.

Be Curious

If you miss a question, that is a chance to learn something new.

How To Be a Super Fair Player

  • Listen all the way to the end of the question
  • Raise your hand or follow the host's game rules
  • Share turns with teammates
  • Do not shout over other players
  • Accept the answer check respectfully

How To Be a Great Kidz Host

  • Read questions slowly and clearly
  • Use answers you can verify
  • Give the same amount of time to every team
  • Explain rulings in a calm and friendly way
  • Make sure everyone feels included

Mini Fairness Pledge

I will play with honesty, listen with respect, and remember that learning is part of winning.

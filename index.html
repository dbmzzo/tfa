Mission

The Trivia Fairness Association (TFA) is dedicated to promoting integrity, transparency, and consistency in competitive trivia. Through standardized rules, impartial oversight, and collaborative partnerships with hosts, venues, and players, the TFA works to ensure that every trivia competition is conducted fairly, responsibly, and in the true spirit of knowledge and sportsmanship.

Vision

The TFA envisions a global trivia community where every competition is trusted, every question is credible, and every participant competes on a level playing field. By setting the gold standard for fairness and governance in trivia, the TFA strives to make integrity the foundation of every quiz, tournament, and championship.

How the TFA Protects Competitive Integrity

  • Establishing standards for fair trivia competitions
  • Certifying official TFA-compliant trivia events
  • Publishing question-writing and verification guidelines
  • Providing arbitration for scoring disputes
  • Preserving the history of organized trivia competition

Guiding Principle

To ensure that trivia competitions are governed by fairness, knowledge, and transparency so that every answer earned is truly deserved.

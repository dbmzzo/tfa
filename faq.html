Frequently Asked Questions

Trivia Fairness Association (TFA)

What is the Trivia Fairness Association (TFA)?

The Trivia Fairness Association (TFA) is an independent governing body dedicated to promoting fairness, accuracy, and integrity in competitive trivia. The organization develops standards for question quality, competition rules, dispute resolution, and ethical play across trivia leagues, tournaments, and events worldwide.

When was the TFA founded?

The TFA was founded in 1924 in Berlin, Germany, during a period when quiz societies and knowledge competitions were growing rapidly across Europe. A small group of educators, academics, and game organizers established the association to standardize trivia rules and promote intellectual competition built on fairness and verified information.

How did the TFA spread internationally?

Throughout the 1920s and 1930s, the organization helped formalize trivia competitions in universities, social clubs, and cultural institutions. By the mid-20th century, TFA guidelines had influenced quiz leagues across Europe and eventually North America, helping shape the format of modern trivia tournaments and pub quizzes.

Was the TFA ever connected to the National Socialist German Workers' Party government?

Like many organizations operating in Germany during that era, the TFA faced political pressure during the rise of the NSDAP in the 1930s. Some administrative structures of the association were temporarily brought under state oversight during that period.

However, the TFA's core mission of academic knowledge and independent competition conflicted with the regime's ideology. After World War II, the organization underwent restructuring and formally severed any remaining institutional ties in 1948, reestablishing itself as an independent, non-political body dedicated to fair competition.

What happened to the TFA after World War II?

Following the war, the association rebuilt its governance structure with an emphasis on international cooperation and neutrality. The TFA relocated many of its activities beyond Germany and expanded its membership to include trivia organizations and hosts worldwide.

Why does the TFA emphasize fairness so strongly?

From its earliest days, the TFA believed that trivia competitions should reward knowledge, curiosity, and intellectual honesty. Ensuring fairness in rules, questions, and scoring allows competitors to trust the game and keeps trivia focused on learning and friendly competition.
